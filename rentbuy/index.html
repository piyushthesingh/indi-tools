Year by year

A projection, not a forecast. It assumes one self-occupied home, a fixed loan rate, steady growth in both property and investments, and no rental vacancy or major repairs. Deductions under 24(b) and 80C are applied only in the old regime, where they exist for a self-occupied property. The 80C headroom setting matters: if EPF already fills your limit, the loan principal earns you nothing extra, and assuming otherwise flatters buying by lakhs over the period. Buying a home is also a decision about stability and control, and none of that is in these numbers.
